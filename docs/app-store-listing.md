# App Store listing draft

## App name

Thai Tax Invoice – PDF & Email

## Tagline

Collect Thai buyer tax details and automatically issue seller-branded Full Tax Invoice PDFs from Shopify.

## Description

Make Thai tax-invoice requests simple for your customers and your team. Thai Tax Invoice lets shoppers enter their Tax ID, branch number, registered address, and invoice email from the cart or checkout. After the order is created—or fulfilled, depending on your setting—the app creates a numbered A4 PDF and emails it to the buyer.

Built for Thai Shopify merchants:

- Collect individual or company tax-invoice details in cart and checkout.
- Configure seller name, registered address, Tax ID, branch number, VAT rate, and invoice numbering.
- Choose when to issue: after order creation, after fulfillment, or manually from the admin.
- Generate Thai + English Full Tax Invoice PDFs with VAT-inclusive or VAT-exclusive calculations.
- Email the PDF automatically through your verified email domain.
- Add your company logo, representative signature image, and company stamp image.
- Review pending requests, issued invoices, and downloadable PDFs in the embedded admin.

Start with a 14-day trial on paid plans. No separate checkout or external billing is required; plan charges are handled by Shopify App Pricing.

This app generates seller-issued Full Tax Invoice PDFs. It does not claim to create Revenue Department e-Tax Invoice & e-Receipt documents. Signature and stamp uploads are visual branding only. Confirm the final invoice fields, VAT treatment, numbering, retention, and email process with your Thai accountant.

## Demo-store walkthrough

1. Install the app and open Settings.
2. Enter seller legal details and choose `Issue after order creation`.
3. Upload a logo, signature, and stamp image.
4. Add the Tax Invoice block to the cart theme and activate the checkout extension.
5. Place a test order with the request checkbox enabled.
6. Open Requests & invoices and download the generated PDF.
